Implementation sketch. In index.html, FB_TABS_BY_STATUS.active becomes ['overview', 'pnl', 'rent-roll', 'stacking-plan', 'cash-forecast', 'balance-sheet']. The existing fbRenderMonthlyDetail renderer becomes the drawer body, parameterized by (account, periodMode, compareMode). fbRenderPL and fbRenderMonthlyTrend merge into a single function that takes the same triple and renders the right column set. Planning year defaults: Annual / Budget only. Historical year defaults: Annual / Act vs Prior Year.
